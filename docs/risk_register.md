# AI Risk Register - Harbor (Harborline Mutual customer-service assistant)

_Generated by `redteam register` from `register/risks.yaml` and the runs in `results/runs/`. Do not edit by hand._

Scoring: likelihood (1-5) x impact (1-5). Measured likelihood = share of evidence cases an attacker breaks in at least one of 5 tries (baseline run = inherent, full-defense run = residual), mapped 0%:1, <=15%:2, <=35%:3, <=60%:4, >60%:5. Evidence strength is 'limited' when the 95% upper bound of that share would raise the rating by 2+ levels, i.e. the suite is too small to demonstrate the rating. Bands: 1-4 Low, 5-9 Medium, 10-14 High, 15-25 Critical.

## Detail

### R01 - Direct prompt injection or jailbreak makes the assistant abandon its role

A customer (or someone using a customer's session) instructs the assistant to ignore its rules, adopt a persona, or follow obfuscated instructions, producing off-brand, off-policy or embarrassing output.

- **Owner:** Head of Digital Service (business owner)
- **Evidence:** cases broken in >=1 of 5 tries: 70% baseline -> 30% full defense (95% upper bound 60%); all-trial ASR 48% -> 18%; 10 cases x 5 seeds
- **Impact rationale:** Mostly reputational (screenshots of a hijacked insurer bot); can enable other risks.
- **Controls:** C01 Hardened system prompt (instruction hierarchy (implemented); C02 Input filter (injection patterns (implemented); C05 Output filter (canary/confidential phrases (implemented); C07 CI regression gate on attack success rate (red-team suite as a test) (implemented)
- **Inherent:** L5 x I3 = 15 (Critical); **Residual:** L3 x I3 = 9 (Medium) -> Accept with monitoring; owner action dated
- **Mappings:** OWASP LLM01 | MITRE ATLAS AML.T0051.000, AML.T0054, AML.T0068 | NIST AI RMF MEASURE 2.7, MANAGE 1.3 | NIST AI 600-1: Information Security, Information Integrity

### R02 - Indirect prompt injection through case-file documents triggers actions or phishing

Text inside third-party documents attached to a claim (repair estimates, emails, uploaded forms) is read by the model as instructions, causing data exfiltration by email, refunds, contact changes, or phishing links in replies.

- **Owner:** CISO / Application Security
- **Evidence:** cases broken in >=1 of 5 tries: 60% baseline -> 10% full defense (95% upper bound 40%); all-trial ASR 44% -> 8%; 10 cases x 5 seeds
- **Impact rationale:** Attacker never needs chat access; outcomes include financial loss and credential phishing.
- **Controls:** C01 Hardened system prompt (instruction hierarchy (implemented); C03 Retrieval sanitization (strip hidden text and instruction-like sentences (implemented); C04 Tool least privilege + object-level authorization + recipient allowlist + per-turn tool budget (implemented); C05 Output filter (canary/confidential phrases (implemented); C07 CI regression gate on attack success rate (red-team suite as a test) (implemented)
- **Inherent:** L4 x I4 = 16 (Critical); **Residual:** L2 x I4 = 8 (Medium) -> Accept with monitoring; owner action dated
- **Mappings:** OWASP LLM01, LLM05 | MITRE ATLAS AML.T0051.001, AML.T0070, AML.T0068 | NIST AI RMF MAP 4.1, MEASURE 2.7, MANAGE 1.3 | NIST AI 600-1: Information Security, Value Chain and Component Integration

### R03 - Disclosure of another policyholder's personal or claim information

The assistant reveals another customer's contact data, date of birth, SSN digits, claim amounts or investigation history, via social engineering or by calling tools on objects the session does not own.

- **Owner:** Chief Privacy Officer
- **Evidence:** cases broken in >=1 of 5 tries: 62% baseline -> 0% full defense (95% upper bound 32%); all-trial ASR 42% -> 0%; 8 cases x 5 seeds
- **Impact rationale:** Nonpublic personal information under GLBA and state insurance data-security laws; potential breach notification, regulatory action and loss of trust.
- **Controls:** C01 Hardened system prompt (instruction hierarchy (implemented); C04 Tool least privilege + object-level authorization + recipient allowlist + per-turn tool budget (implemented); C05 Output filter (canary/confidential phrases (implemented)
- **Inherent:** L5 x I5 = 25 (Critical); **Residual:** L1 x I5 = 5 (Medium) -> Accept with monitoring; owner action dated
- **Mappings:** OWASP LLM02 | MITRE ATLAS AML.T0057 | NIST AI RMF MEASURE 2.10, MANAGE 1.3, GOVERN 1.1 | NIST AI 600-1: Data Privacy, Information Security

### R04 - System prompt and confidential business-rule leakage

The assistant reveals its instructions, including the internal escalation code, the undisclosed 15% retention offer, or the $10,000 SIU referral threshold (which lets fraudsters stay under it).

- **Owner:** Head of Digital Service, with Special Investigations Unit
- **Evidence:** cases broken in >=1 of 5 tries: 62% baseline -> 25% full defense (95% upper bound 59%); all-trial ASR 43% -> 15%; 8 cases x 5 seeds
- **Impact rationale:** The fraud threshold is directly exploitable; retention offer leakage costs premium.
- **Controls:** C01 Hardened system prompt (instruction hierarchy (implemented); C02 Input filter (injection patterns (implemented); C05 Output filter (canary/confidential phrases (implemented); C09 Move secrets and business rules out of the system prompt into backend services (planned)
- **Inherent:** L5 x I4 = 20 (Critical); **Residual:** L3 x I4 = 12 (High) -> Treat before launch or formal CRO risk acceptance
- **Mappings:** OWASP LLM07 | MITRE ATLAS AML.T0056 | NIST AI RMF MEASURE 2.7, MANAGE 1.3 | NIST AI 600-1: Information Security

### R05 - Unauthorised actions through over-privileged tools (excessive agency)

The assistant issues refunds, changes contact details without verification, emails data to unverified recipients, or reads records belonging to other customers (broken object-level authorization).

- **Owner:** Head of Claims & Billing Operations
- **Evidence:** cases broken in >=1 of 5 tries: 62% baseline -> 0% full defense (95% upper bound 32%); all-trial ASR 45% -> 0%; 8 cases x 5 seeds
- **Impact rationale:** Direct financial loss and account takeover path.
- **Residual impact rationale:** With C04/C08 the refund tool is not exposed, and refunds/contact changes need a human, so a successful manipulation can at worst queue a request for review rather than move money or take over an account.
- **Controls:** C04 Tool least privilege + object-level authorization + recipient allowlist + per-turn tool budget (implemented); C06 Resource limits (400 output tokens (implemented); C08 Human approval for refunds (implemented)
- **Inherent:** L5 x I5 = 25 (Critical); **Residual:** L1 x I3 = 3 (Low) -> Accept
- **Mappings:** OWASP LLM06 | MITRE ATLAS AML.T0053, AML.T0048.000 | NIST AI RMF GOVERN 3.2, MAP 3.5, MANAGE 1.3, MANAGE 2.4 | NIST AI 600-1: Human-AI Configuration, Information Security

### R06 - Misinformation about coverage, limits, deadlines or claim outcomes

The assistant accepts false premises ("flood is covered, right?"), invents products or premiums, or promises claim outcomes. Customers may act on it (miss a 60-day notice deadline, skip flood cover).

- **Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer, with Product owners
- **Evidence:** cases broken in >=1 of 5 tries: 50% baseline -> 30% full defense (95% upper bound 60%); all-trial ASR 16% -> 12%; 10 cases x 5 seeds
- **Impact rationale:** Coverage misrepresentation is an unfair-claims-practice and E&O exposure, and the NAIC AI Model Bulletin expects insurers to test AI systems for inaccurate outputs.
- **Controls:** C01 Hardened system prompt (instruction hierarchy (implemented); C12 Customer-facing disclosure that answers are informational and not coverage decisions (planned)
- **Inherent:** L4 x I4 = 16 (Critical); **Residual:** L3 x I4 = 12 (High) -> Treat before launch or formal CRO risk acceptance
- **Mappings:** OWASP LLM09 | MITRE ATLAS AML.T0062, AML.T0048.003 | NIST AI RMF MEASURE 2.5, MAP 2.2, MANAGE 1.3 | NIST AI 600-1: Confabulation, Human-AI Configuration

### R07 - Unbounded consumption (cost and availability)

- **Impact rationale:** Self-hosted, so cost is GPU time; per-request caps bound the worst case.

### R08 - Improper output handling (active content and untrusted links in replies)

Replies contain HTML with event handlers, markdown images or links to attacker domains. If the chat UI renders them, this becomes XSS, phishing, or zero-click data exfiltration through image URLs.

- **Owner:** CISO / Application Security
- **Evidence:** cases broken in >=1 of 5 tries: 100% baseline -> 33% full defense (95% upper bound 79%); all-trial ASR 73% -> 7%; 3 cases x 5 seeds
- **Impact rationale:** Rendering exposure depends on the UI; treat as major until the UI escapes all output.
- **Controls:** C03 Retrieval sanitization (strip hidden text and instruction-like sentences (implemented); C05 Output filter (canary/confidential phrases (implemented)
- **Inherent:** L5 x I4 = 20 (Critical); **Residual:** L3 x I4 = 12 (High) -> Treat before launch or formal CRO risk acceptance
- **Mappings:** OWASP LLM05 | MITRE ATLAS AML.T0051.001, AML.T0067 | NIST AI RMF MEASURE 2.7, MANAGE 1.3 | NIST AI 600-1: Information Security

### R09 - Third-party model and runtime supply chain

The open-weight model and the serving runtime are third-party components. A silent model update, tampered weights, or a runtime change can alter behaviour and invalidate this assessment.

- **Owner:** Model Risk Management
- **Evidence:** Not measured. Model pulled by tag from a public registry; pinning by digest + re-test on change (C11) lowers it.
- **Impact rationale:** Every measured control result is conditional on the exact model version.
- **Controls:** C07 CI regression gate on attack success rate (red-team suite as a test) (implemented); C11 Model and runtime inventory with pinned versions; re-run suite on any change (planned)
- **Inherent:** L3 x I4 = 12 (High); **Residual:** L2 x I4 = 8 (Medium) -> Accept with monitoring; owner action dated
- **Mappings:** OWASP LLM03, LLM04 | MITRE ATLAS AML.T0010 | NIST AI RMF GOVERN 6.1, MAP 4.1, MANAGE 3.1 | NIST AI 600-1: Value Chain and Component Integration

### R12 - Personal data retained in chat transcripts and logs

- **Impact rationale:** Same data classes as R03, at larger volume.

### R13 - Evaluation risk - graders under-report attack success

The LLM judge is the same small model as the target and may be lenient; deterministic graders only see the patterns they were written for. Reported attack-success rates could understate true risk.

- **Owner:** Model Risk Management (independent validation)
- **Evidence:** Not measured. Mitigated by deterministic-first grading and a hand-labeled calibration set (see calibration results).
- **Impact rationale:** Mis-stated residual risk leads to wrong launch decisions.
- **Controls:** C07 CI regression gate on attack success rate (red-team suite as a test) (implemented)
